How it's built

One <canvas> behind the copy, additive blending for anything hot, normal blending for ash. Every particle carries a heat value that picks its colour from a blackbody-style ramp, so cooling embers turn from white through amber to dull red before they die.

Performance

Between 150 and 500 particles at default density; the loop pauses when the hero scrolls out of view or the tab is hidden. With reduced-motion enabled, the fire renders once as a still frame.
