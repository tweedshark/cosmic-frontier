Assumptions and limits

  • This uses the flat 2% formula you specified. OMERS' actual lifetime pension is integrated with CPP — roughly 1.325% on earnings up to the average YMPE plus 2% on earnings above it — which produces a lower lifetime figure than a flat 2%. Treat this console as a planning ballpark, not as an OMERS statement.
  • Service accrues continuously at one year per calendar year from the anchor date, capped at the max-credited-service value above.
  • The best-five average compounds smoothly at your growth rate rather than stepping once a year, so the live figure moves every second instead of jumping each January.
  • Milestone dates assume you keep working continuously. Age and qualifying service each climb a year per year, so the factor gains two points annually. Qualifying service for the factor is not capped, even though credited service stops at 35.
  • Nothing here is indexed, reduced, or taxed. No early-retirement reduction, no bridge benefit, no post-retirement inflation indexing, no survivor-option cost, and no income tax are applied. Your 30-year target should qualify for an unreduced pension, so no reduction is the right assumption there — but it also means the bridge benefit payable to 65 is missing from these figures.
  • Your inputs are saved in this browser only (localStorage). Nothing is transmitted anywhere.
